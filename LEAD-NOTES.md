## 🔴 Compliance headlines — the short version

Full tables are in `PROMPT.md`. These are the four that will bite.

1. 🔴🔴 **OZONE.** Ozone is a lung irritant; the **EPA** has published that ozone generators sold as air cleaners are not effective at concentrations meeting public health standards. Any claim it **kills / sanitises / disinfects** is a pesticidal and health claim under **FIFRA** and the FTC Act. **Odour treatment only.** Banned words, everywhere including alt text and meta: kills, sanitise, disinfect, sterilise, germs, bacteria, virus, mould, pathogen, antimicrobial, medical grade, hospital grade, air purification.
2. 🔴 **NO PRICES.** None are published. Every figure is `[PRICE — NOT PUBLISHED, CLIENT TO SUPPLY]`. Build the card and tier structure complete; invent nothing.
3. 🔴 **CERAMIC COATING DURABILITY.** "9H", "5 years", "permanent", "self-healing" are FTC Act claims needing substantiation — and the substantiation belongs to the coating manufacturer, not the installer. `[COATING DURABILITY — MANUFACTURER-SUBSTANTIATED FIGURE REQUIRED, CONFIRM]`.
4. 🔴 **"LICENSED & INSURED WITH THE STATE OF FLORIDA"** is their claim, repeatable as theirs, with **no number of any kind**. ⚠️ **Florida does not licence auto detailing as a trade** — most likely they mean a county/city business tax receipt or occupational licence. Confirm what they mean before it goes on a page in those words.

**Also:** no paint-correction outcome guarantee · no damage-free promise on PPF/vinyl removal · no environmental claim of any kind (FTC Green Guides) · "open 24 hours" is not operating hours · no marque, badge or visible plate anywhere · no minors in imagery · no review scores and no `aggregateRating` · ⛔ no photo from their GBP (real customers' cars, no consent, identifying plates).

## ⚠️ Water reclamation — ask it, and ask it as a commercial question

A mobile detailer discharging wash water to a street or storm drain is **regulated in many Florida jurisdictions under stormwater / NPDES rules**. Glaze says they bring their own water; **where it goes afterwards is a separate question and nobody has answered it.**

**Why it matters commercially, which is how to raise it:** containment and reclamation is the reason a property manager, office park, dealership or municipal lot lets a detailer work on their asphalt when they turn away everyone else. If Glaze reclaims, it belongs on the page as **capability**. If they don't, we need to know before we aim the site at commercial work.

⛔ **It is never framed as "eco" or "green"** — that is a Green Guides problem and it is also a weaker argument.

## 🔴 The location-page content job — the question list to put in front of them

Twenty pages that differ only in the city name is the **doorway-page** pattern. It gets suppressed, and it is worse than having no pages because it is sitewide. **Real content has to come from the person who drives there.**

**Per area, ask:**
1. What do you actually run into in `<Area>`? Gated communities, HOA rules on driveway work, condo decks, apartment car parks, downtown parking garages, office parks, no shade?
2. Which of your services does `<Area>` actually buy most? (⛔ We do not assume this — ask.)
3. Is it inside your core radius, or does it carry a travel charge / minimum job?
4. Roughly how often are you there — weekly, monthly, by request?
5. Anything specific about the vehicles you see there?
6. **One before/after photo from that area.** One per area is a realistic ask and it is what makes the page unarguably genuine.
